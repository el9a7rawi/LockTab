LockTab

Your tabs. Your eyes only.

Nothing leaves your browser

LockTab has no server, no account and no analytics. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to sync. Every byte it keeps lives in this browser, on this device.

Your PIN is never stored as text

We derive a key with PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 using 600,000 iterations and a fresh 16-byte salt. Only that hash and its settings are saved. The same applies to your one-time recovery code.

What the extension reads

Only the address of the current tab, so we can decide whether that page should be covered. We keep the origin and path as a key to remember what you locked; the query string and fragment are dropped, the full URL is never stored, and nothing is transmitted. Page content is never read.

Why it asks for these permissions

Storage keeps your settings and hashes locally. Tabs identifies the page you are covering. Scripting draws the lock over it. Alarms wakes the extension when a temporary unlock expires. All sites is necessary because any tab you open later may need locking — no page content is collected.

What LockTab is honest about

It hides a page from someone who sits down at your desk. It is not disk encryption, and it cannot stop a compromised browser or an administrator who removes the extension. Your PIN is yours to keep; if you lose it, only the recovery code you saved can bring it back.
